Our Story

A House Built on
Considered Dressing

VELAIRE began with a simple belief: that modesty and modernity are not opposites. Each piece is designed in small batches, cut from breathable natural fibres, and finished by hand — so that covering more never means expressing less.

Our ateliers work season after season on a single idea — quiet luxury that moves with you, from the first prayer of the morning to the last light of evening.
